## Interview guide

Mom Test rules (Fitzpatrick): ask about the past, never pitch, never ask "would you use…?".

1. Tell me about the last time you had to decide whether a customer got money back. What happened, step by step?
2. What was the hardest part of that one? How long did it take?
3. Where did you have to look things up? How many places?
4. When you got one wrong, how did you find out? What happened next?
5. Tell me about a time you said no to a refund. How did you word it, and what came back?
6. If you could have changed one thing about that moment, what would it be?

**Do not ask:** "Would you use an AI that decides refunds?" · "Do you like this panel?" ·
"How much would you pay?" · "Do you think merchants would trust this?"

**What we are listening for**, because it maps to things our brief already claims:
- how many systems they open per decision (our as-is process says two, at 5 of 9 minutes);
- whether they can reconstruct an old decision months later (our whole case for the receipt);
- whether a wrongful refusal ever came back to them at all (our claim that it is silent);
- what they did when the evidence contradicted the customer (our EV-016).
