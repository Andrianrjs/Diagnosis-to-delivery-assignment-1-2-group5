# Session 8 — Launch and pitch — what to do

**Before Tuesday 24 November**

1. Read `S8_Launch_and_Pitch_READ_AT_HOME.pptx`.
2. Build your slides from your repo. Slide 6 of the document gives a possible plan in ten slides, and slides 7 to 9 explain each one with the example of Paylio. The plan is a suggestion: the order, the number of slides and the style are your choice.
3. Prepare your demo, or a short video of it. Decide who says what.

**On Tuesday 24 November, in class**: teams that want to can rehearse their pitch in front of the class (8 minutes of pitch, 3 minutes of feedback). It is not compulsory, and nothing is graded. Everyone works on their project.

**On Thursday 26 November, on Git**

4. Write `08_launch_memo.md` from the template in this folder. One page, six parts. Slides 3 to 5 of the document explain each part, with the example of Paylio.
5. Add the Session 8 line to `governance.md` (`governance_line_S8.md`).
6. Push the final versions of `spec.md` and `03_product_brief.md`, then tag your repo: `git tag v1.0`. Your pitch slides and your demo video do not go on Git.

**On Friday 27 November**: 8 minutes of pitch, then 3 minutes of questions. Everyone in the team speaks.
