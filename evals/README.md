## The golden set and how it is graded

The golden set is EV-021 to EV-050, the eval-v0 held-out cases. It never changes. The receipts in `grading_golden30.csv` come from the eval-v0 rules, re-run on 20 November 2026 with the first written text of the policy (`kb/refund_policy.md`, version `2026-11-v1`) so that the receipts carry the clause text. The decisions are identical to `results_v0.csv`.

1. Grader A (Raveloarimanga Rajesy Ny Andrian) and grader B (Clémence Marco) fill their verdict column **independently**, without talking, with one sentence when the verdict is not EXECUTE. Grader B graded with Claude's reading of the rubric as support (20 November 2026), so column B is not a fully independent human grade: the human–human rate below must be read with that in mind.
2. The judge grades the same 30: paste each block of `judge_inputs_golden30.txt` into a **fresh** chat (one with no access to this repo or to our labels), copy the verdict into `judge_verdict`.
3. `python3 evals/agreement.py` gives the two rates and the rows to read by hand.
4. We read the disagreements, fix the guideline or the prompt **once**, and write below what changed.

## Known biases, stated

- The two graders wrote the labels of EV-021 to EV-050 on 17 November: they know the expected answers, which can inflate the human–human rate.
- The labels of EV-051 to EV-100 were proposed by the same AI that drafted the guideline and the policy text: an error in its reading of the policy would appear in both, and the eval would not catch it.
- The guideline, the judge prompt and the policy text were drafted with an AI (Claude) and reviewed by us. If the judge is a Claude model, it shares a model family with the drafter of the guideline. The agent itself has no model (rules only), so there is no self-preference on the agent's output.
- The judge must not see the expected labels: `grading_golden30.csv` and the prompts do not contain them.

## Cadence

Eval-set v1 and the judge at every change of the rules, the policy version or a model · a human sample of the L4 decisions every week · the two rates re-measured at each policy change.
